What it keeps track of

Every page of the ledger, handled.

Not just logging — the full job a bookkeeper would do for you, in one WhatsApp thread.

01

Text, voice, or receipt photo

Say it out loud, type it, or snap the bill. One AI model reads all three — no separate scanning app.

02

Replies in your language

English, Roman Urdu, Urdu script, or Punjabi — MaaliDost mirrors whichever one you used, every time.

03

Budgets that warn you

Set a daily, weekly, or monthly limit per category. Get nudged before you go over, not after.

04

Bill reminders that fire themselves

Set it once, in any language. A daily sweep sends it on the due date — no app to remember to open.

05

Made a mistake? Just undo it

Misspoke or mistyped an amount? Say "undo" and resend the correct one. No menus to dig through.

06

A real, exportable report

Ask for your report — get a CSV or PDF with your full history, balance summary, and budget status. Urdu text renders correctly, right to left.

07

Savings goals

Set a target — "goal: save 20000 for Eid" — and track progress automatically as your balance grows. No separate "add to goal" step to remember.

08

Streak tracking

Log a few days in a row and MaaliDost notices — a small nudge that keeps the habit going.
